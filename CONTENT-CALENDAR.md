# Jesus Worldwide: 90-Day Content Calendar

**16 November 2026 to 14 February 2027 · 13 weeks**
Goal: restart the channel with a steady rhythm of **1 long-form video and 4 Shorts every week**, built first from the 4 songs we already have, then from new songs, local-language versions and church collaborations.

---

## The weekly rhythm

| Day | What | Time (Ghana / GMT) |
|---|---|---|
| **Mon** | Short: chorus or scripture hook | 6:00 pm |
| **Wed** | Short: behind the scenes, story or challenge | 6:00 pm |
| **Fri** | **Long-form video** (Premiere) | 7:00 pm |
| **Sat** | Short: cut from Friday's video | 10:00 am |
| **Sun** | Short: worship or prayer moment, for church day | 7:00 am |

Why this works: Friday evening is the start of the weekend, Sunday morning catches people on their way to church, and each Saturday Short sends viewers to the video posted the day before.

### Rules for every upload
- **Category: Music.** Paste the description template from [`METADATA-FIXES.md`](METADATA-FIXES.md), including lyrics, the scripture, streaming links and a question for comments.
- **Hashtags:** use 3 at most in a Short's title and up to 7 in a description. Use **#JesusWorldwide #AfroGospel** plus song-specific tags (#GatesOfPraise, #HowGreatYouAre, #MysteriesOfPraise, #GhanaGospel, #PraiseAndWorship). **Never #nsppd or #elroi.**
- **Shorts:** 15 to 45 seconds, vertical (1080×1920), with on-screen lyrics in the first second and **Related video** linked to the full song.
- **Thumbnails:** use the brand files in [`brand/thumbnails/`](brand/thumbnails/). New designs come from [`brand/thumbnails.html`](brand/thumbnails.html).
- **AI disclosure:** tick *Altered or synthetic content* whenever visuals or vocals are AI-generated.
- **Pinned comment** on every long-form video: the scripture plus "Where are you watching from? 🌍"

## Church collaboration plan
1. **List 10 churches** (local churches, campus fellowships and youth ministries) and their worship leaders.
2. **Offer them a free session:** a live recording of one of our songs with their choir, credited to their church in the title.
3. **Give them the files:** the video, plus a 60-second cut for their own social pages and WhatsApp status.
4. **Return visit:** offer a Sunday ministration slot.
5. **Aim for 1 collaboration a month.** The first is in Week 11.

## Production batching (to keep up the pace)
- **One recording day per fortnight.** Film all the Shorts for 2 weeks in one go: chorus performances, talking heads, behind the scenes.
- **Lyric videos:** use one template (brand colours and fonts, lyrics synced to the song) and change only the background per song.
- **Compilations:** build them in an editor from the 4 songs + instrumentals + scripture cards. Make 1 hour once, then cut 45 and 30 minute versions.
- **Every long-form video gives at least 2 Shorts.**
