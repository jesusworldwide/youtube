## 2. The monetization targets (YouTube rules as of Nov 2026)

YouTube announced on 10 Aug 2026 that the requirements change on **1 Feb 2027** ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/new-youtube-creators-face-8-000-hour-bar-for-ad-revenue-from-february-2027/), citing YouTube's announcement). Check [YouTube's official YPP page](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72851) before applying.

| Level | What it unlocks | Requirement | Changes in 2027? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fan funding** (first target) | Channel memberships, Super Thanks, Super Chat in lives, Shopping | **500 subscribers** + **3,000 watch hours** (last 365 days) **or** 3 M Shorts views (last 90 days) | **No change** |
| **Ad revenue** (second target) | Ads on videos, YouTube Premium revenue | **1,000 subscribers** + **8,000 watch hours** (365 days) **or** 20 M Shorts views (90 days) from 1 Feb 2027 | Doubled from 4,000 h / 10 M |

**Honest timeline:** reaching 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 hours before 1 Feb 2027 (11 weeks away, from 15 subscribers and 11 hours) is not realistic, so we plan for the **2027 rules**:

| Milestone | Target date | What it needs |
|---|---|---|
| 100 subscribers, 250 watch hours | **end of Jan 2027** | Daily Shorts + weekly long-form start in December |
| 500 subscribers, 3,000 watch hours → **fan funding** | **mid 2027** | Long worship/Bible-story videos doing the heavy lifting |
| 1,000 subscribers, 8,000 watch hours → **ad revenue** | **late 2027 to early 2028** | Same engine, doubled, plus collaborations |

### The watch-hour maths
- 8,000 hours = **480,000 minutes**. Shorts views **do not** count toward watch hours.
- A 4-minute song watched for 2 minutes needs **240,000 views** to get there. That's too slow.
- A **1-hour worship / soaking compilation** watched for 15 minutes on average needs **32,000 views**. People play these while praying, working or sleeping.
- A **10-minute Bible story** watched for 6 minutes needs **80,000 views**, and families rewatch these.

So: **Shorts bring people in; long videos earn the hours.**

| 6. Publish metadata | Claude via the YouTube API: titles, descriptions, tags, category, thumbnails, playlists | `youtube-mcp` |
| 7. Review | Claude pulls analytics **every Monday** and adjusts the plan | YouTube Analytics API |

*Uploading video files through the API is a possible next upgrade to the tools; for now, upload in Studio and Claude does everything else.*

### Rules that protect the channel's future income
1. **Authentic, not mass-produced.** YouTube refuses monetization for channels of templated, near-identical or fully automated videos ("inauthentic content" policy; [Metricool summary](https://metricool.com/youtube-ai-content-rules-monetization/)). We use **real voices, real faces, original scripts and varied formats**. AI helps with drafting, artwork and music, but people make the ministry.
2. **Disclose AI** every time: set **Altered content → Yes** in Studio (the API can't set it).
3. **Scripture: KJV or WEB only** in on-screen text and descriptions. Both are public domain. NIV, ESV and NLT are copyrighted.
4. **Music rights:** songs must come from a **paid Suno plan** before monetizing or distributing to Spotify, Audiomack or Boomplay.
5. **No borrowed audiences:** no #nsppd, #elroi or other ministries' tags; no reuploaded sermons or footage.
6. **Every long video:** chapters, lyrics or scripture in the description, a pinned comment, an end screen to the next video, and a playlist.

## 6b. Going international: Africa and the diaspora

**Target markets:** 🇬🇭 Ghana (home base) · 🇳🇬 Nigeria · 🇰🇪 Kenya · 🇺🇬 Uganda · 🇿🇦 South Africa · 🇬🇧 UK · 🇺🇸 US · 🇦🇺 Australia · 🇨🇦 Canada, plus English-speaking Christians everywhere.

**Why it matters twice over:** more nations reached with the Gospel, *and* viewers in the UK, US, Canada and Australia earn the channel **several times more per view** once ads are on.

### 1. Post at times that work across time zones

| Our slot (GMT) | Lagos | Nairobi / Kampala | Johannesburg | London | New York | Sydney |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **6:00 am** Daily Bread | 7 am | 9 am | 8 am | 6 am | 1 am* | 5 pm |
| **7:00 pm** long videos | 8 pm | 10 pm | 9 pm | 7 pm | 2 pm | 6 am (next day) |

\*Shorts keep being recommended for days, so the US catches up. Use **Premieres** for big releases so every time zone sees a countdown. (These are November times; from late March, add 1 hour for London and New York and take 1 off for Sydney.)

### 2. Titles that anyone in the world understands
- Lead with universal search words, such as *worship songs, praise and worship, Bible story, Christmas worship, African gospel*, and keep "Ghana" for descriptions and tags.
- Rotate pan-African tags: **#AfricanGospel #AfroGospel #WorshipSongs #ChristianMusic** alongside #GhanaGospel.
- Keep the Ghanaian flavour *inside* the content (accents, "Eii", trotro, Makola). Audiences love authenticity; they just need to be able to find us first.

### 3. Speak more languages (cheaply)
- **Captions on every video** (upload or correct YouTube's auto-captions). YouTube can then auto-translate subtitles for viewers anywhere.
- **Translated titles and descriptions** (YouTube Studio → *Languages*) for **Swahili** (Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania), **French** (francophone Africa), **Portuguese** (Angola, Mozambique, Brazil) and **Spanish**. Claude can write these and set them through the API (a small tool upgrade).
- **Songs with local-language choruses** after the Twi song:
  - **Swahili**, e.g. *"Asante Yesu"* (Thank you, Jesus), for East Africa.
  - **Yoruba or Nigerian Pidgin** for Nigeria.
  - **Zulu** for South Africa.

  A native speaker must check every line before recording, as with the Twi song.

### 4. Collaborate country by country
One collaboration per market over the year, with a choir, youth fellowship or gospel creator in Lagos, Nairobi, Kampala, Johannesburg and London. Each one introduces the channel to a new audience.

### 5. Reach the diaspora through churches
Ghanaian, Nigerian, Kenyan and South African churches in the **UK, US, Canada and Australia** are close-knit and active on WhatsApp. Offer them songs, devotionals and Bible Stories free for services and children's church.

### 6. Seasons that unite everyone
Christmas, Easter, New Year and Watch Night are big across all our markets. Add **US Thanksgiving** (4th Thursday of November) and **Mother's / Father's Day** content. Each country's independence day can be a moment for a **"pray for [country]" Short** (e.g. Kenya 12 Dec, Nigeria 1 Oct, Ghana 6 Mar).

### 7. Measure it
The Monday analytics report includes **views and watch time by country**, so we can see which markets respond and lean into them.

## 7. Helping the less privileged: doing it right

Generosity is the goal of growth. It has to be **trustworthy** from day one:

1. **Register officially** as a charity or non-profit in Ghana before asking the public for money, and keep a **separate bank or mobile-money account** for giving.
2. **Be transparent:** publish what came in and what it did (a simple quarterly report on the website).
3. **Show real impact, with dignity:** short videos of outreach (feeding, school supplies, hospital visits) *with consent*, never exploiting people's hardship for views.
4. **Never promise blessings in return for giving.** Give because Christ gave.
5. **Income streams, in order:** fan funding (memberships, Super Thanks) → ads → devotional book sales or donations → music streaming → church and ministry bookings.
6. **Start now with what we have:** a monthly outreach with a few volunteers, filmed respectfully. It's good content and it's the mission.

*YouTube's own fundraising tools are only available to verified non-profits in some countries, so confirm availability for Ghana once registered.*
