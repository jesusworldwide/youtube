# Daily Bread with Jesus Worldwide · December 2026

**Series:** Daily Bread with Jesus Worldwide (YouTube Shorts, @JesusIsWorldwide)
**Posting time:** 6:00 am GMT, every day, 1–31 December 2026
**Season:** Advent (began Sun 29 Nov) → Christmas Eve (Thu 24) → Christmas Day (Fri 25) → Watch Night / New Year's Eve (Thu 31)
**Scripture:** every quote is from the King James Version (KJV), which is in the public domain. Read the verse exactly as written.

**How to film:** Shoot vertical at 1080x1920 (9:16), on a phone held steady or on a small tripod, in natural morning light where you can. Record a real human voiceover: one of the team reading the script calmly and warmly, like you are talking to one friend. Never use an AI voice. Read it through twice before recording, then record in a quiet room (a wardrobe full of clothes makes a good sound booth). Keep every Short under 45 seconds; the scripts below run 30–45 seconds at a relaxed pace. Turn captions on (burn them in or use YouTube auto-captions and correct them), put the hook on screen in the first second, and use brand colours for the text cards. Film your own footage around Ghana (street, market, home, church, sunrise) so each video looks and feels different. Change the location, angle and speaker from day to day so no two uploads look the same.
