# Daily Bread with Jesus Worldwide · November 2026

**Series:** Daily Bread with Jesus Worldwide (YouTube Shorts, @JesusIsWorldwide)
**Posting time:** 6:00 am GMT, every day, 14–30 November 2026
**Season:** Thanksgiving and gratitude → "Gates of Praise" (Psalm 100) → US Thanksgiving (Thu 26) → First Sunday of Advent (Sun 29) → bridge into the December Advent series (Mon 30)
**Scripture:** every quote is from the King James Version (KJV), which is in the public domain. Read the verse exactly as written.

**How to film:** Shoot vertical at 1080x1920 (9:16), on a phone held steady or on a small tripod, in natural morning light where you can. Record a real human voiceover: one of the team reading the script calmly and warmly, like you are talking to one friend. Never use an AI voice. Read it through twice before recording, then record in a quiet room (a wardrobe full of clothes makes a good sound booth). Keep every Short under 45 seconds; the scripts below run 30–45 seconds at a relaxed pace. Turn captions on (burn them in or use YouTube auto-captions and correct them), put the hook on screen in the first second, and use brand colours for the text cards. Film your own footage around Ghana (street, market, home, church, sunrise) so each video looks and feels different. Change the location, angle and speaker from day to day so no two uploads look the same.

### Tue 17 Nov · Did You Know? · Hallelujah
- **Title:** The Word Every Language Already Knows #shorts #BibleFacts
- **Verse:** "Let every thing that hath breath praise the LORD. Praise ye the LORD." (Psalm 150:6, KJV)
- **On-screen hook (first 1 second, max 7 words):** One word, every language.
- **Script (spoken):** Did you know? "Hallelujah" is a Hebrew word. "Hallelu" means praise ye, and "Jah" is a short form of God's name. So Hallelujah simply means "Praise ye the LORD." It's one of the few words Christians say in almost every language on earth. In Accra, in Lagos, in Nairobi, in London, the same word rises. The book of Psalms ends with it: "Let every thing that hath breath praise the LORD. Praise ye the LORD." If you have breath this morning, you qualify. Comment "Hallelujah" and tell us which country you're in.
- **On-screen text beats:**

  - Jah = the LORD

- **Visual idea:** Quick cuts of different people (market seller, student, grandmother, mechanic) each saying "Hallelujah" to camera.
- **Description:** Hallelujah means "Praise ye the LORD," and it is spoken by believers all over the world.

### Tue 24 Nov · Did You Know? · Jehovah Jireh
- **Title:** Where the Name Jehovah Jireh Comes From #shorts #BibleFacts
- **Verse:** "And Abraham called the name of that place Jehovahjireh: as it is said to this day, In the mount of the LORD it shall be seen." (Genesis 22:14, KJV)
- **On-screen hook (first 1 second, max 7 words):** We sing it. Where's it from?
- **Script (spoken):** Did you know? We sing "Jehovah Jireh" in churches all over Africa, but the name was first spoken on a mountain. On Mount Moriah, God provided a ram in Isaac's place. Then the Bible says, "Abraham called the name of that place Jehovahjireh: as it is said to this day, In the mount of the LORD it shall be seen." It means "the LORD will see to it," the LORD will provide. And one day, on a hill near there, God provided His own Son. Comment "Jehovah Jireh" if God has seen you through.

- **Description:** Jehovah Jireh, "the LORD will provide," was first named by Abraham on Mount Moriah (Genesis 22:14).
