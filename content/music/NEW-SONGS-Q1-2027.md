### Music video concept (low budget, Ghana)

- **Location:** A church compound or open courtyard (Accra or Kumasi) dressed with simple Christmas touches: white backdrop cloth, a wooden manger with straw, paper stars and fabric bunting in brand colours.

## 3. Meda Wo Ase (Thank You, Yesu)

**Language:** Twi chorus, English verses.

> **Important: native-speaker check required.** Every Twi line below must be checked by a fluent native Twi speaker (spelling, tone, grammar and natural phrasing) before recording, and again after hearing the Suno vocal, because AI vocals often mispronounce local-language words. The phrases were kept deliberately short and common.

**Twi phrases used (with English meaning):**

| Twi | English |
|-----|---------|
| Yesu | Jesus |
| Meda wo ase | I thank you |
| Ayeeyi | Praise |
| Ayeeyi ma Yesu | Praise to Jesus |
| Onyame | God |

- **Scripture foundation:** Psalm 136:1 (O give thanks unto the LORD; for he is good); 1 Thessalonians 5:18; Psalm 103:1–5; Revelation 7:9–10 (every nation, kindred, people and tongue) (KJV)

  8. Finale: everyone gathered in the compound singing the outro, wide drone or high-angle shot if available.

### 10-minute extended worship version (structure)

Aim for about 10–11 minutes. Generate the base song in Suno, then extend sections (Suno "extend") or loop in an editor:

| Time | Section |
|------|---------|
| 0:00–1:00 | Spoken intro over pads (Matthew 11:28) |
| 1:00–4:00 | Verse 1, Pre-Chorus, Chorus, Verse 2, Pre-Chorus, Chorus |
| 4:00–6:00 | Bridge "All of me, all for You", repeated 4–6 times, building |
| 6:00–8:30 | Soaking section: instrumental with free singing and choir hum |
| 8:30–10:00 | Final chorus, quiet then full, then twice more |
| 10:00–10:45 | Outro and amen, pads fade |

Video for the extended version: slow, calm visuals (see concept) with lyrics on screen, so people leave it playing during prayer. Add chapters in the description (Intro, Song, Bridge, Soaking, Final Chorus).

```
Slow Afro worship ballad, intimate and prayerful, soft piano, warm pads, gentle guitar, light percussion, tender male lead, soft choir harmonies, surrender
```

### Music video concept (low budget, Ghana)

- **Location:** An empty church sanctuary at dawn, or an open field or beach (e.g. near Cape Coast or Ada) at sunrise. One singer, a keyboardist, and 3–4 choir members.
- **Colour palette:** Mostly white (clothing, drapes, sheer fabric moving in the breeze); soft blue and teal for calm; touches of orange and yellow from sunrise light; red and green used sparingly (a red cloth laid down as a "surrender" symbol, green grass).
- **Shot list:**
  1. Opening: slow shot of sunrise over the field or sea, spoken Matthew 11:28.
  2. Close-up of open, empty hands lifted (Verse 1 "nothing in my hands").
  3. Lead singer kneeling in white, eyes closed, singing softly.
  4. Symbolic shot: someone laying down a heavy bag or stone on the ground and walking away lighter (chorus).
  5. Keyboardist and choir in white with teal and blue sashes, wide and still.
  6. White fabric drapes blowing in the wind, slow motion (bridge).
  7. Soaking section: long, slow drifting shots of worshippers praying, the sea or sky, light through windows.
  8. Final shot: the singer standing, arms open, sun fully risen, fade to white with "Here I Lay It Down."

### Shorts ideas

1. **"Lay it down" moment:** the bag-drop shot with the chorus, text: "What do you need to lay down today?"
2. **Bridge loop:** "All of me, all for You" sunrise clip, a calming prayer Short that links to the 10-minute version.
3. **Scripture + piano:** Matthew 11:28 on screen with the soft intro, ending "Full 10-min worship on our channel."

## Release checklist

For every song:

1. **Lyric video first.** Publish the lyric video at the start of the release window.
2. **Official video 2–3 weeks later.** Use the lyric video's response to promote the official video, and link the two to each other (end screens, pinned comment).
3. **Lyrics in the description.** Paste the full lyrics into every video description (and the Twi with its English translation for "Meda Wo Ase").
4. **Tick "Altered content" in YouTube Studio.** The songs are produced with AI (Suno), so declare altered/synthetic content on upload.
5. **Check Suno commercial rights.** Before monetising, or distributing to Spotify, Audiomack and Boomplay, confirm the Suno plan used to generate each song allows commercial use (songs made on a free plan may not).
6. **Register with a distributor.** Register each song with a music distributor so it reaches the streaming platforms and royalties are collected.
7. **Native-speaker check (Song 3).** A fluent Twi speaker must approve every Twi line before recording and again after hearing the generated vocal.
8. **Hashtags.** Always #JesusWorldwide #AfroGospel plus song tags; never #nsppd or #elroi.
