# The Worship Life: Teaching Series Plan

**Ministry:** Jesus Worldwide (YouTube @JesusIsWorldwide)
**Format:** Weekly long-form teaching, 12 episodes, 12 to 20 minutes each
**Audience:** Ghana, West Africa and worldwide; believers of every denomination and seekers
**Scripture versions on screen:** KJV or WEB only (public domain)

## The name

We recommend keeping **"The Worship Life"**. It is short, easy to say in any accent, works as a playlist title and a hashtag, and it carries the heart of the series: worship is not only a song on Sunday, it is a whole life lived before God (Romans 12:1).

Alternative if you want a stronger link to the music: **"Through the Gates: The Worship Life"**, using "Gates of Praise" as the doorway into the series. Use this as a season subtitle if you like, but keep "The Worship Life" as the main brand so later seasons can follow (for example, "The Worship Life: Season 2, Prayer").

## Series purpose

To teach ordinary believers, in plain and warm language, what the Bible says about thanksgiving, praise and worship, and to help them practise it in daily life: at home, at work, in the market, in the trotro and in the church. Every episode points to Jesus Christ, the One through whom we come to the Father (John 14:6; Hebrews 13:15). Where it is natural, an episode ends by introducing one of our songs so the teaching and the music strengthen each other.

## Episode structure (every week)

| Segment | Length | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0:00 to 0:15 | One striking line or question. No logo intro before it. |
| Welcome and series reminder | 0:15 to 0:45 | "Welcome to The Worship Life..." and the passage for today. |
| Story or illustration | 1 to 2 min | Ghanaian or everyday life; relatable to the worldwide audience. |
| Passage read aloud | 1 to 2 min | Read slowly; passage shown on screen in KJV or WEB. |
| Three points | 8 to 12 min | Each with a practical application. |
| Response and prayer | 2 min | Lead viewers in a simple prayer. |
| Call to action and song link | 1 min | Subscribe, comment, share; introduce the song. |

## The 12 episodes

### Episode 1: Enter With Thanks
- **Passage:** Psalm 100
- **Linked song:** "Gates of Praise"
- **Big idea:** Thanksgiving is the God-given doorway into His presence, and every believer can walk through it today, whatever they are carrying.
- **Main points:**
  1. Thanksgiving is an invitation for everyone: "all ye lands" (v. 1 to 2).
  2. Thanksgiving begins with knowing who God is and whose we are (v. 3).
  3. Thanksgiving rests on God's unchanging character: good, merciful, faithful (v. 4 to 5).
- **Shorts to clip:**
  1. *~00:00 to 00:45* (the hook and the story of the gate) Hook: "Every gate has a key. The key to God's gate is in your mouth."
  2. *~08:00 to 09:00* (Point 2, "you are not self-made") Hook: "You are not self-made. And that is the best news you will hear today."
  3. *~12:45 to 13:45* (Point 3, thanks in hard seasons) Hook: "Your situation changes. His mercy does not."

### Episode 2: Forget Not All His Benefits
- **Passage:** Psalm 103:1 to 5
- **Linked song:** "Gates of Praise" (bridge: "My soul will not forget")
- **Big idea:** Forgetfulness kills gratitude, so worship is the daily discipline of remembering what God has done in Christ.
- **Main points:**
  1. Talk to your own soul: worship is a decision before it is a feeling (v. 1 to 2).
  2. Count the benefits: forgiveness, healing, redemption, love, renewal (v. 3 to 5).
  3. The greatest benefit is forgiveness through the cross; start there.
- **Shorts to clip:**
  1. *~01:00* Hook: "Sometimes you have to preach to yourself before you can praise."
  2. *~06:00* Hook: "Write down five things God did this year. I dare you to stop at five."
  3. *~11:00* Hook: "If God did nothing else for you, the cross is enough."

### Episode 3: How Great You Are
- **Passage:** Psalm 145:1 to 13
- **Linked song:** "How Great You Are"
- **Big idea:** Praise is telling the truth about God's greatness, and His greatness is never exhausted.
- **Main points:**
  1. God's greatness is "unsearchable": we will never reach the end of it (v. 3).
  2. Praise is passed from one generation to the next (v. 4 to 7).
  3. His greatness is gentle: gracious, full of compassion, good to all (v. 8 to 9).
- **Shorts to clip:**
  1. *~00:00* Hook: "You will spend eternity discovering God and never get to the end."
  2. *~07:00* Hook: "Your grandmother's praise is the reason you know God. Who will know Him because of you?"
  3. *~12:00* Hook: "The greatest God is also the gentlest."

### Episode 4: Thanks in Every Season
- **Passage:** Habakkuk 3:17 to 19; 1 Thessalonians 5:16 to 18
- **Linked song:** "Gates of Praise" (chorus: "I will give You thanks in every season")
- **Big idea:** We give thanks *in* every circumstance, not always *for* every circumstance, because God remains our joy when everything else fails.
- **Main points:**
  1. Habakkuk's honest list of losses: faith does not pretend.
  2. "Yet I will rejoice": joy anchored in God, not in the harvest.
  3. "In every thing give thanks": a will-of-God practice for ordinary believers.
- **Shorts to clip:**
  1. *~02:30* Hook: "The Bible never asks you to pretend you are fine."
  2. *~08:00* Hook: "Two little words that change everything: 'Yet I will.'"
  3. *~13:00* Hook: "In everything, not for everything. That difference matters."

### Episode 5: Spirit and Truth
- **Passage:** John 4:19 to 26
- **Linked song:** "Mysteries of Praise"
- **Big idea:** The Father is looking for worshippers, and true worship is not about the right mountain but the right heart, led by the Spirit and shaped by the truth.
- **Main points:**
  1. Worship is not owned by one place, tribe or style.
  2. "In spirit": from the inside, by the Holy Spirit.
  3. "In truth": anchored in who God really is, revealed in Jesus.
- **Shorts to clip:**
  1. *~01:30* Hook: "God is not looking for worship. He is looking for worshippers."
  2. *~06:30* Hook: "Your village, your city, your church style: none of it is the point."
  3. *~12:00* Hook: "Fire without truth burns out. Truth without fire goes cold."

### Episode 6: The Sacrifice of Praise
- **Passage:** Hebrews 13:15 to 16
- **Linked song:** "How Great You Are"
- **Big idea:** Because Jesus offered the final sacrifice, our response is a continual offering of praise with our lips and goodness with our hands.
- **Main points:**
  1. "By him": we only come to God through Jesus.
  2. "Continually": praise when it costs us something.
  3. "To do good and to communicate": worship that shows up in generosity and kindness.
- **Shorts to clip:**
  1. *~02:00* Hook: "Praise is easy when life is easy. That is not a sacrifice."
  2. *~08:00* Hook: "Some of your best worship will happen in your kitchen."
  3. *~13:30* Hook: "If your song does not reach your neighbour, check your song."

### Episode 7: Songs at Midnight
- **Passage:** Acts 16:22 to 34
- **Linked song:** "Gates of Praise"
- **Big idea:** Praise in the darkest hour is a testimony that God sees, and others are listening.
- **Main points:**
  1. Paul and Silas praised before anything changed.
  2. "The prisoners heard them": your praise has an audience.
  3. The jailer's salvation: God's purpose was bigger than the prison.
- **Shorts to clip:**
  1. *~01:00* Hook: "What do you do at midnight?"
  2. *~07:30* Hook: "Somebody is listening to how you handle your hard season."
  3. *~13:00* Hook: "The earthquake was not the miracle. The jailer was."

### Episode 8: Praise Goes First
- **Passage:** 2 Chronicles 20:1 to 22
- **Linked song:** "How Great You Are"
- **Big idea:** When we do not know what to do, we can turn our eyes to God and let praise lead the way, trusting the battle to Him.
- **Main points:**
  1. Fear is real, but Jehoshaphat "set himself to seek the LORD" (v. 3).
  2. "Our eyes are upon thee" (v. 12): praying when we have no plan.
  3. Singers went before the army (v. 21 to 22): praise as trust, not a technique.
- **Shorts to clip:**
  1. *~02:00* Hook: "The most honest prayer in the Bible: 'We don't know what to do.'"
  2. *~08:00* Hook: "Put your eyes where your help is."
  3. *~13:30* Hook: "Praise is not a trick to get God to move. It is trust that He already has."

### Episode 9: Mysteries of Praise
- **Passage:** 1 Corinthians 14:13 to 19; Romans 8:26 to 27
- **Linked song:** "Mysteries of Praise"
- **Big idea:** The Holy Spirit helps us pray and sing beyond our own words, and Scripture calls us to pray and sing with the spirit and with the understanding also, for the building up of the church.
- **Main points:**
  1. When words fail, the Spirit helps our weakness (Romans 8:26).
  2. "With the spirit, and with the understanding also" (1 Corinthians 14:15): heart and mind together.
  3. The goal is edification and love: everything done to build others up (14:12, 26; chapter 13).
- **Teaching note:** Keep this episode pastoral and inclusive. Teach the text plainly, honour believers from every tradition, and keep the focus on the Spirit's help, Christ's glory and love for the church.
- **Shorts to clip:**
  1. *~01:30* Hook: "Ever been in so much pain you could not find words to pray?"
  2. *~07:00* Hook: "Paul's balance: spirit and understanding. Not one or the other."
  3. *~13:00* Hook: "If it does not build up your brother, it is not the point."

### Episode 10: A Living Sacrifice
- **Passage:** Romans 12:1 to 2
- **Linked song:** "Gates of Praise" (verse 2: "Every word I speak, every step I take")
- **Big idea:** In view of God's mercy, our whole everyday life, our body, work and choices, becomes our worship.
- **Main points:**
  1. "By the mercies of God": worship is a response, not a payment.
  2. "Present your bodies": worship at work, in the market, in the classroom.
  3. "Be ye transformed": a renewed mind that wants God's will.
- **Shorts to clip:**
  1. *~02:00* Hook: "Your work shift can be a worship service."
  2. *~08:00* Hook: "God does not want your Sunday. He wants your Monday too."
  3. *~13:00* Hook: "You cannot worship like heaven and think like the world."

### Episode 11: Psalms, Hymns and Spiritual Songs
- **Passage:** Colossians 3:15 to 17
- **Linked song:** All three songs (mini playlist)
- **Big idea:** When the word of Christ lives richly in us, it overflows in songs that teach, encourage and unite God's people.
- **Main points:**
  1. "Let the word of Christ dwell in you richly": worship starts with Scripture.
  2. Singing together teaches and admonishes: worship is a family activity.
  3. "Whatsoever ye do... giving thanks": the whole of life under the name of Jesus.
- **Shorts to clip:**
  1. *~01:30* Hook: "The songs you sing are teaching you something. What?"
  2. *~07:30* Hook: "When was the last time your family sang together?"
  3. *~12:30* Hook: "Do everything in the name of Jesus. Even that."

### Episode 12: Every Nation Before the Throne
- **Passage:** Revelation 7:9 to 12 (with Psalm 100:1, "all ye lands")
- **Linked song:** "Gates of Praise" (reprise, closes the season)
- **Big idea:** Worship has a destination: a great multitude from every nation, Ghana included, praising the Lamb together forever, and we are invited to bring others with us.
- **Main points:**
  1. A multitude no one could number, from every nation, tribe, people and tongue.
  2. "Salvation to our God... and unto the Lamb": heaven's song is about Jesus.
  3. Jesus is worldwide: our worship becomes witness today.
- **Shorts to clip:**
  1. *~01:00* Hook: "Heaven will sound like every language on earth."
  2. *~07:00* Hook: "Your Twi, Ga, Ewe, Hausa or English praise will be in that choir."
  3. *~13:30* Hook: "Don't go to heaven alone. Bring someone."

## Filming guidance

**Setting**
- One teacher on camera, seated or standing, in a simple, clean Ghanaian setting: a verandah with plants, a room with a kente or adinkra-print cloth on the wall, a wooden table with an open Bible, or a church interior during the day.
- Keep the background uncluttered and consistent for the whole series so viewers recognise it. Add a small brand element (Jesus Worldwide logo print or the series title on a canvas) off to one side.
- Avoid busy roads and generator noise. Film early morning or evening when it is quieter, and check for ceiling fans and AC hum.

**Light**
- Face a large window or doorway for soft natural light, or use two inexpensive LED panels with diffusers at 45 degrees either side of the camera.
- Do not film with a bright window behind the teacher.
- Lock white balance and exposure so the skin tone stays natural and consistent across episodes.

**Camera**
- 16:9 for the main teaching; frame from mid-chest up with the eyes on the upper third.
- Shoot at 4K if possible so 9:16 vertical Shorts can be cropped without losing quality. Keep the teacher centred so vertical crops work.
- A second angle (a side shot or a close-up of the Bible) is optional; use it for cutaways and to hide edits.

**Sound**
- Lapel (lavalier) microphone on every shoot, clipped about a hand's width below the chin, cable hidden.
- Record a backup on a phone or recorder. Record 10 seconds of room silence for noise reduction.
- Music under the teaching only at the start and end, never under speech; use our own songs (instrumental versions) to avoid copyright claims.

**Captions**
- Upload accurate English captions (SRT) for every episode; do not rely on auto-captions for scripture and names.
- Burn-in large, high-contrast captions on Shorts (most viewers watch muted).
- When budget allows, add French captions for francophone West Africa, then Twi.

**Scripture on screen**
- Show every passage on screen as it is read, labelled with the reference and version (KJV or WEB).

**Chapter markers**
- Put chapters in every description, starting at 0:00, with at least three chapters, each 10 seconds or longer.
- Standard chapters: Hook, Welcome, Story, The Passage, Point 1, Point 2, Point 3, Prayer, Song and Next Steps.

**Shorts workflow**
- Mark the three Short moments during filming (the teacher can briefly pause before and after each hook line).
- Cut each Short to 30 to 59 seconds, open on the hook line, end with "Full teaching on the channel" and link the long video as the related video.

**Consistency**
- Same day and time each week (for example, Thursdays 6 pm GMT).
- Same thumbnail template: teacher's face, three to four words of title, series badge "The Worship Life", episode number.
- Hashtags: #JesusWorldwide #BibleTeaching #TheWorshipLife. Never use #nsppd or #elroi.
