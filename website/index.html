Jesus Worldwide Tools

Jesus Worldwide Tools is a private, internal application used only by the Jesus Worldwide ministry team to manage our own YouTube channel, @JesusIsWorldwide.

With the channel owner's permission (granted through Google sign-in), it uses YouTube API Services to:

  • read our channel's videos, playlists and statistics;
  • update our own video titles, descriptions, tags, categories and thumbnails;
  • manage our channel description, keywords and playlists;
  • read our channel's YouTube Analytics reports so we can plan content.

It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account. Read how we handle data in our Privacy Policy and the rules of use in our Terms of Service.
