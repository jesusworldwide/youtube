Privacy Policy

Effective 10 November 2026

This policy explains how Jesus Worldwide ("we", "us") handles information in connection with our website and with Jesus Worldwide Tools (the "App"), a private application our ministry team uses to manage our own YouTube channel through YouTube API Services.

1. Who the App is for

The App is used only by authorised members of the Jesus Worldwide team to manage the @JesusIsWorldwide YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public.

2. Information the App accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google and grants permission, the App can access:

  • YouTube channel data: channel name, description, keywords, trailer setting and public statistics;
  • Video and playlist data: titles, descriptions, tags, categories, thumbnails, privacy status and statistics for our own uploads and playlists;
  • YouTube Analytics data: aggregated reports for our channel, such as views, watch time, subscribers, traffic sources, countries and devices.

The App requests these Google OAuth scopes: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/yt-analytics.readonly. It does not access Gmail, Google Drive, contacts or any other Google service. Analytics data is aggregated and does not identify individual viewers.

3. How we use it

  • to view and report on our channel's performance;
  • to update our own videos' titles, descriptions, tags, categories and thumbnails;
  • to manage our channel description, keywords and playlists.

We do not use this information for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to build profiles of any person.

4. AI assistance

Our team runs the App through an AI assistant (Claude, provided by Anthropic). When a team member asks the assistant to review or update the channel, the relevant channel data is sent to that assistant only to carry out that request. Google user data is not used to train generalised AI or machine-learning models.

5. Storage and security

The App runs on our own team computer. The Google sign-in token is stored locally on that device with restricted file permissions and is never published or shared. We do not operate a server database of Google user data. Reports are kept only as long as needed for our planning.

6. Sharing

We do not share, transfer or disclose Google user data to third parties, except: (a) to the AI assistant as described above, to perform a requested task; (b) when required by law; or (c) with the user's explicit consent.

7. Google API Services User Data Policy

Jesus Worldwide Tools' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

8. YouTube API Services

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

9. Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke the App's access at any time in your Google Account's third-party connections page. When access is revoked, we delete the locally stored token. To ask us to delete any other data we hold, email us at the address below and we will do so within 30 days.

10. Our website

This website does not use cookies, analytics trackers or advertising. Our hosting provider (Vercel) may process standard server logs, such as IP addresses, to deliver and secure the site.

11. Children

The App is not directed at children and is used only by our adult ministry team.

12. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above will change when we do.
